1. The name and the words

HOW THE ISLE IS NAMED AND HOW IT SPEAKS

  • The house and the place are called The Isle, with a capital T, in every use, including mid sentence and the possessive, The Isle’s.
  • The street address never appears in anything created, presented, published or filed, including file names. Everything is just The Isle.
  • Regalia is the builder’s plan and model name. It appears only inside source documents, never as the home’s name in anything presented, written or published.
  • The register: high end, elusive, exclusive, and at the same time barefoot luxury, down to earth and easy going. Nothing boasts and nothing sells. Quality is stated plainly and warmly; confidence comes through restraint and ease.
  • No em dashes, anywhere, in messages or in anything created.

6. Spacing and layout

THE MEASURES OF THE TWO SETTLED COMPOSITIONS

  • The index is a split, two columns at 46 and 54 fractions, no outer frame. One hairline divides the panels, inset 52px at the top and at the bottom. Panel padding: left 52, 46, 30; right 34, 50, 78. On phones the panels stack and the divider and the chart are hidden.
  • The lockup sits at the top of the left panel, width min(252px, 72%). The tagline sits 26px below it, then the tagline rule, 56px wide, 1px, gold at .75 opacity. The report begins 40px below.
  • Entries: a 96px numeral column, gap 8px by 22px, padding 30px, 14px, 28px. The numeral is Playfair Display 500 at 58px. The status line is a 10.5px label preceded by a 26px gold rule.
  • The splash: masthead 112px (78 short, 100 narrow, 74 both); the wordmark at clamp(38px, 6.7vmin, 56px), letterspacing .34em; a short centred silver rule, clamp(140px, 28vw, 240px); poster mat padding clamp(10px, 3vmin, 28px); poster aspect 2 to 3, six seconds each, crossfade 1.2 seconds, always starting on the first poster.
  • Nothing on any page requires sideways scrolling, at any width.

7. The chart

SOUNDINGS IN FATHOMS · DRAWN TO ITS OWN SPEC

  • One coastline, a fine double shore line in gold, one gentle curve with a single soft bay, the land above it empty. No anchor, no hachures, no headlands, no names or labels.
  • Three depth curves follow the shore as true parallel offsets, at +54, +108 and +162 units. Soundings on a disciplined grid in five bands: 2, 3, 4 · 5, 6, 8 · 10, 13, 16 · 20, 24, 28 · and 34 alone at the centre, deepest and lowest.
  • Motion is gentle and on the water only. Under reduced motion the chart is still. PLATE I stands in the frame’s bottom right.

9. Rules for creating anything

THE DISCIPLINE THAT KEEPS THE GUIDE TRUE

  • This guide governs. One approved sample precedes any full round. Alternatives differ in composition, organisation and visual world, never in cover imagery alone.
  • Every file created is audited against its source and this guide, fixed, and re-audited in full until one complete pass finds zero defects, verified in the rendered result at the size the user will see it.
  • No substitution of anything the user chose or supplied, without asking.
  • Publishing is by git over SSH, never by browser upload. Deletions are enumerated first and removed in one commit, on the user’s approval of the specific items.
  • Anything created is stripped of metadata. Where an author field is required it reads MDS.
  • When a settled value changes, this guide is updated in place, at this name, and the change is recorded in the separate change log.

11. Technical requirements

HOW THE SITE STANDS

  • GitHub Pages, repository 1s1e/1s1e.github.io, branch main, published by git over SSH with the repository’s dedicated key.
  • Visitor counting is GoatCounter, one counter per website; this site’s counter is 1s1e, reported only on new activity, with locations.
  • robots.txt allows the landing page and its /assets/ only.
  • Locked pages are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2-SHA256 at 250,000 iterations, decrypted in the visitor’s browser. Passwords are never published, filed or recorded.
  • Pages with a toggle open in dark mode. Both modes are always built and always checked.
