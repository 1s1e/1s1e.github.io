1Name and the words

How The Isle is named and how it speaks

  • The house and the place are called The Isle, with a capital T, in every use, including mid sentence and the possessive, The Isle's.
  • The street address never appears in anything created, presented, published or filed, including file names. Everything is just The Isle.
  • Regalia is the builder's plan and model name. It appears only inside source documents, never as the home's name in anything presented, written or published.
  • The Isle is a single residence, not a multi-structure facility, with unique named rooms, among them the Map Room, the Lanai, the Theater, the Studio and the Guest Suite.
  • The register: high end, elusive, exclusive, and at the same time barefoot luxury, down to earth and easy going. Nothing boasts and nothing sells. Quality is stated plainly and warmly; confidence comes through restraint and ease. The Isle is the ultimate destination, a cut above the rest, and says so by how it is made, not by claiming it.
  • The line of the house: "More than a place you come to, a place you stay." It is set in Playfair Display, italic, weight 500, wherever it stands, and its words are exact, never paraphrased.
  • Page titles: the splash is titled The Isle. Interior pages follow the form The Isle · <Name>: The Isle · The Build, The Isle · Reference Catalogues, The Isle · Secure. The 404 page carries Page Not Found · 1s1e.com.
  • Words in use: dates are written in full, "November 22, 2026", and times as "7:21 AM"; a date and a time are joined by the middot, the house separator. Labels are uppercase, letterspaced. Buttons and titles are in Title Case; body text and descriptions are in sentence case.
  • Units and numbers: measurements are feet and inches; elevations and levels are given to two decimal places; thousands are separated by commas; a figure and its unit are joined by a space.
  • No em dashes, anywhere, in messages or in anything created.

  • Text stands in its settled pairings: The Isle Ivory and Ivory Soft on The Isle Navy, Panel and Deep; The Isle Navy and Soft Ink on The Isle Ivory and Light Tint. Gold text is Page Gold, dark or light by the mode.
  • Text pairings meet a contrast floor: 4.5 to 1 for body text, 3 to 1 for large text. Dim Ink on The Isle Ivory (3.4 to 1) and Page Gold light on The Isle Ivory (3.1 to 1) are large text pairings only; every dark pairing in use passes the body floor.
  • Colour in proportion: the ground carries the page. The Isle Gold is reserved to labels, eyebrows, rules, dots, the switch's knob, the coastline of the chart and small marks of that kind; it never fills a field or a panel and it is never a ground. The Isle Green appears only inside the artwork.
  • The ground rule: in each mode, the theme colour, the body ground and the page gradient's first and last stops are the one ground colour, The Isle Navy in dark and The Isle Ivory in light, checked along the page's whole length.
  • Colour misuse is shown, not only stated: Plate B of the book shows The Isle Gold filling a field, The Isle Green used as a surface, a colour outside the sixteen set as a ground, and body text set in Dim Ink on The Isle Ivory, below the contrast floor.

4Logo and the artwork

Five colourways · The artwork itself, never redrawn

The parts, named exactly: the logo is the island, the palm and the words together. The mark is the island and the palm, without the words. The palm alone is an element of the artwork, never the logo and never the mark. The anatomy never changes: the crown is The Isle Green, the trunk is The Isle Gold, and the island, its waterline and the text follow the colourway. In dark mode the island and the text are The Isle Silver, and that stays. In the colour artwork for light grounds, the island and the text are The Isle Navy.

  • The wordmarks are Playfair Display, weight 500, held as master artwork, never set as live page text and never redrawn. Copies for a site or for print are issued from the master file set, never recoloured locally.
  • The mark is never redrawn, never recoloured outside the five colourways, never stretched, rotated, outlined or shadowed, and never placed on a ground that fights it. Logo misuse is shown, not only stated: Plate A of the book shows the lockup stretched, squashed, recoloured outside the colourways, set on a ground that fights it and tilted, and the palm lifted out and set alone as if it were the logo.
  • Clear space: on all sides of a lockup, three quarters of the height of the mark in the lockup. Standing alone, the mark keeps clear space of half its own height. The two measures are not interchanged.
  • Minimum sizes: the horizontal lockup never below 160px wide on screen or 40mm in print; the vertical lockup never below 90px tall or 22mm; the mark never below 24px tall or 6mm. Below the mark's minimum, the icon serves instead.
  • The mark stands alone where the words are already given, or where they will not fit. The palm stands only in its settled places: the icon, the seal, the profile picture, whole and uncropped on white or The Isle Ivory, and the catalogue footer, flanking The Isle on both sides. Beyond them it is not decoration; in rare circumstances it may serve as an ornament, taken deliberately, never as a habit or a motif.

7Chart

Soundings in fathoms · A fragment of an engraved chart

  • The chart is the house's signature graphic, drawn to one plate, viewBox 640 by 400. It is set in its own block in open ground, never behind text. One coastline, a fine double shore line in gold, entering and leaving the edges in the upper third, with a point and a bay, the land above it empty. No anchor, no hachures, no headlands, no names or labels. The plate carries no frame and no compass rose.
  • Three depth curves follow the shore as true parallel offsets, at +54, +108 and +162 units, dotted, in ivory. The soundings are taken along four survey lines run off the shore, deepening along each line in small steps: 2, 5, 11 and 19 down the first line, 3, 7, 14 and 23 down the second, 2, 6, 13 and 22 down the third, 4, 9 and 17 down the last, every figure clear of the curves. Sounding figures are Playfair Display, italic, 500, 13px.
  • Motion is gentle and always on the water only: the depth curves drift on staggered cycles of 13, 17 and 21 seconds, the soundings pulse on 10, 14 and 18 seconds, the coastline never moves. Under reduced motion the chart is still. Sound follows the same discipline: nothing sounds uninvited, and sound and music stand off by default.

9Rules for creating anything

How things are created, and what is required of them

How things are created:

  • This guide governs everything created for The Isle, without exception. A value, a name or a practice that is not in this guide is not settled, and anything that departs from it is drift, to be corrected.
  • No page hard-codes anything: anything created for the site takes its shared layer from the master style sheet, published at /assets/the-isle-master.css, and links it as published. A page carries none of the shared values as its own copy: not the colour variables, not the font stacks or the type roles, not the switch, the button and field forms, and not the link colours. What a page keeps is its layout and what is specific to it.
  • An exception to the shared layer is never written into a page: where a section must depart from the master, the exception lives in that section's own folder, as the section's style sheet, layered on the master, and the master itself is unchanged. This page's own sheet, style.css in this folder, is the standing example.
  • Images follow the same rule: one version of each image, at one address, and every page that uses it references that copy, so an image is updated in one place and the update is universal. An image specific to a section lives in that section's folder.
  • One approved sample precedes any full round of creative work. Samples are true samples, built from the real pages and their real style sheet, shown in every state the decision turns on, in both modes, at full quality. Alternatives differ in composition, organisation and visual world, never in cover imagery alone.
  • No substitutions without asking: an image, a file, a product, a design element or wording the user chose or supplied is never replaced without asking.
  • Corrections propagate everywhere the old value appears, and each is verified in place. A file that has been presented is never silently overwritten: a changed version is reissued under a fresh version name.
  • Publishing is by git over SSH from the local clones, never by browser upload, and a publish is verified the Bear LP way: the served source fetched directly and hash compared, a nonexistent path fetched for the served 404, and a dashboard hit treated as final proof where a dashboard exists. Deletions are enumerated first and removed in one commit. Nothing is published, sent or deleted without the user's approval of the specific item.

What is required of anything created:

  • Every page carries the full favicon set, the master colours and the master type only. A page does not keep private colours or a private typography.
  • Both modes are always built and always checked. Nothing requires sideways scrolling, at any width.
  • Every file created is audited against its source and this guide, fixed, and re-audited in full, repeating until one complete pass finds zero defects. Layout is verified in the rendered result, at the size the user will see it, and every page is checked at phone width, at iPad portrait and landscape, and at desktop width, in the engine the user actually uses.
  • Anything created is stripped of metadata. Where an author field is required it reads MDS. Nothing created carries any credit, attribution or other reference to its making.
  • Every interactive element shows a visible focus, and every image carries alt text.

10References

Precedents, sources of truth, and the documents

Precedents, researched November 2026, for the private site's guest experience: the Four Seasons Residences app and the Four Seasons app itinerary and chat; Aman; Six Senses; Belmond; Cheval Blanc Randheli; Waldorf Astoria Residences; and the great private islands, North Island, COMO Laucala and Necker Island. The recognition principle: at this level there are no points and no tiers; the guest is known.

Sources of truth, in order: this guide; the master file set and its MANIFEST.md; the live pages at 1s1e.com; the local publishing clone. The master documents are kept to endure, at fixed names, one canonical copy in each master file set, updated in place as things change; their history is kept separately, in CHANGELOG.md, never inside the documents themselves. This guide changes only on the user's word.

Documents and print

  • A document follows the book's own format: US Letter, margins of 56pt at the sides and the foot and 44pt at the top, no running head, and a running foot of one line in the label form naming the document "THE ISLE · <TITLE>" at the left of the column, the folio at the right. Covers stand on The Isle Navy, with the mark in its silver with colour tree colourway.
  • A PDF carries MDS as its author of record, with producer and creator blank. In print, the sixteen are built as CMYK from the tabulated builds in section 2 and proofed against the master values on the stock in use. The Isle Gold and The Isle Silver print as flat colour, never as metallic foil.
  • The catalogue PDFs are finished print documents with their own settled interior design. The site presents them; the site palette does not govern their contents.

Correspondence

  • A personal closing takes one of two forms: The Isle with M & D Silver beneath it, the imprint form, or The Isle alone.
  • House email, from the house's own address, is signed The Isle Staff: one line, in Title Case, standing as the whole closing.

Imagery

  • The house is shown in its own photography, photographed as it stands, in full colour and untreated in character. Where the subject is inspiration rather than the house, curated photographs from resorts and hotels around the world may be used, captioned as what they are. Artwork leads where artwork is the subject, as the posters lead the splash.
  • No stock imagery, and no image of another house is ever presented as The Isle. Image files for pages are issued from the masters at the size they will be shown: photographs as JPEG or WebP, flat artwork as PNG or WebP, each stripped of metadata.

11Technical standards

How the site stands

  • The site is GitHub Pages, repository 1s1e/1s1e.github.io, branch main, published by git over SSH with the repository's dedicated key.
  • The master style sheet is published at /assets/the-isle-master.css, byte identical to the copy in the master file set, and every page on the site links it. Where a section departs from the shared layer, its exception is kept in that section's own folder, as the section's style sheet, layered on the published master.
  • Each image stands at one address and is referenced from there by every page that uses it, so an update to an image is made once and lands everywhere. An image specific to a section is kept in that section's folder.
  • Visitor counting is GoatCounter, one counter per website; this site's counter is 1s1e, reported only on new activity, with the visits' locations. A page that counts by hand loads its tracker without counting, and counts each view once.
  • robots.txt allows the landing page and its /assets/ only.
  • Locked pages are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, the key derived by PBKDF2-SHA256 at 250,000 iterations, decrypted in the visitor's browser by Web Crypto. Passwords are never published, filed or recorded. A locked page shows only its heading, the password field and the Open button; a wrong password is answered with one sentence only: "That password does not open this page." The secure zone is titled The Isle · Secure and headed Secure Test Zone.
  • Pages with a toggle open in dark mode, and the toggle switches the whole page. The one exception: where a page remembers the visitor's choice, the remembered choice stands over the opening default, and otherwise their system preference is followed. Both modes are always built and always checked.
  • The ground rule of section 2 is a check on every page: in each mode the theme colour, the body ground and the page gradient's first and last stops are the one ground colour, and the page is checked along its whole length, at the foot as well as the top.

12Switch and the buttons

One switch, one button, everywhere

The switch at the head of this page is the settled form itself, drawn from the master style sheet: a pill, 117 by 44px, on a Deep ground at 94 percent, with a 1px border of The Isle Gold at 55 percent, brightening to full The Isle Gold on hover. Its knob, 54 by 32px in The Isle Gold, carries the glyph of the mode in use in Deep, rests on the side of that mode, and slides 51px to change, on a .25 second ease. The choice is remembered on the visitor's own device, in localStorage under the key isle-theme.

  • Touch: every control stands at least 44px tall, the Apple standard. The switch stands 44px tall, and the button carries a 44px minimum height.
  • The button is one form everywhere, the form the Reference Catalogues carried: sharp corners, Source Sans 3 at 14.5px, weight 700, letter-spacing 0.1em, the labels written in Title Case and set in capitals by the form, padding 14px by 22px, a 1px border and a 44px minimum height. Off, it is transparent with a border of The Isle Gold in dark and Page Gold light in light, its text in the mode's ink; on hover it fills with that gold and its text turns to Deep in dark and The Isle Ivory in light. On, it fills with The Isle Navy, its text The Isle Ivory, its border The Isle Gold in dark and The Isle Navy in light; on hover the fill lifts to Panel, the border unchanged.
  • One exception: the panel grid buttons of the Lot Viewer and the Shadow Study carry a smaller size, 10.5px on the Lot Viewer and 13px on the Shadow Study, letter-spacing 0.05em and horizontal padding of 12px, so their longest labels sit on one line. Every other part of the form is unchanged.
  • Form fields are one form everywhere: at least 44px tall, Source Sans 3 at 17px, on Panel in dark and Light Tint in light, a 1px border of Page Gold at 30 percent in dark and 38 percent in light, corners of 6px, padding 8px by 12px, and on focus the border brightens with the 2px gold focus outline.
  • A button carries an icon only where the action has a true symbol of its own: the open book for reading, the tray arrow for a download, at 17px before the words with a 10px gap. Symbols are drawn to one specification: on a 24 by 24 grid, with a stroke of 2, round caps and round joins, in the currentColor of their setting, and no fill, save where an established symbol is solid by nature. The switch's sun and moon are the one exception: drawn small inside the pill, their stroke is 1.8.
  • Four symbols stand alone, replacing the words: Menu, Close, Search and Settings, each keeping its name for assistive technology. Project Home, the way back to The Build index, is a control with the home glyph alone, at 20px, with no words. A control that changes state carries the symbol of the state it is in: Menu and Close, Play and Pause, and the switch's sun and moon are pairs of that kind.
  • The assignment rule: the primary action on a page takes the on form, secondary actions take the off form, and utility actions stay text links, not buttons. Read is a button in the on form; Download, Previous and Next, the Reader's Download PDF and Try Again are buttons in the off form; Full Size, Link to This Page, Copy Link and Hide are text links, and stay text. A link inside text stands in Page Gold, dark or light by the mode, and brightens on hover.
