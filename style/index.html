1Type

Two faces, six roles

Two faces only, everywhere on 1s1e.com: Playfair Display for display, headings and serif lines; Source Sans 3 for body, labels and small text. Text pages load the faces once, with exactly this line:

https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Playfair+Display:ital,wght@0,500;0,600;1,500&family=Source+Sans+3:wght@400;600;700&display=swap

The fallback stacks are "Playfair Display", Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif for the serif and "Source Sans 3", "Avenir Next", "Segoe UI", sans-serif for the sans. The splash, whose lettering is artwork, loads Playfair Display 500 only, for its fallback text.

The colours in use

  • Text stands in its settled pairings: The Isle Ivory and Ivory Soft on The Isle Navy, Panel and Deep; The Isle Navy and Soft Ink on The Isle Ivory and Light Tint. Accent text is The Isle Silver in dark and Page Silver in light.
  • Text pairings meet a contrast floor: 4.5 to 1 minimum for body text, 3 to 1 minimum for large text. Page Silver on The Isle Ivory measures 4.57 to 1 and meets the body floor. Dim Ink on The Isle Ivory measures 3.42 to 1, a large text pairing only: in light mode, small secondary text, labels and meta text take Soft Ink, and Dim Ink stands at large sizes only.
  • The floors govern text and the borders that define a control. The settled hairline borders, silver at low alpha dividing and edging the panels, are decorative and stand outside the floor.
  • The accent follows the ground, not the mode: The Isle Silver on dark grounds and Page Silver on light grounds, on every page. Chrome standing on The Isle Navy keeps full silver in both modes.
  • The ground rule: in each mode, the theme colour, the body ground and the page gradient's first and last stops are the one ground colour, The Isle Navy in dark and The Isle Ivory in light, so the browser bars meet the page without a seam. A page is checked along its whole length, at the foot as well as the top.
  • The inversion: in light mode a page turns over. Its bands and its footer exchange ground and ink with their dark form, Panel exchanging with Light Tint and Deep and The Isle Navy with The Isle Ivory, and the accent steps from The Isle Silver to Page Silver, so the light page is the dark page mirrored, not a different page. What is imagery does not invert. Page chrome always inverts.
  • Colour in proportion: the ground carries the page, with Panel and Deep as its steps in dark and Light Tint in light. Silver marks the page: it is reserved to structure, labels, eyebrows, rules, dots, the switch and its knob, the coastline of the chart and small marks of that kind; silver never fills a field or a panel and it is never a ground. The Isle Green and The Isle Gold appear only inside the artwork. A page that gives silver a field, or green or gold a surface, is out of proportion and is corrected.

4The switch

One form everywhere, shown live

  • A pill, 117px by 44px, fully rounded, on a Deep ground at 94 percent, with a 1px border of The Isle Silver at 55 percent, brightening to full The Isle Silver on hover.
  • A sun glyph stands at the left, 11px in, and a moon glyph at the right, both in The Isle Silver at 85 percent. Drawn small inside the pill, their stroke is 1.8, the one exception to the symbols' stroke of 2.
  • The knob is 54px by 32px, fully rounded, in The Isle Silver, carrying the glyph of the mode in use in Deep. It rests on the side of the mode in use and slides 51px to change, on a .25s ease.
  • The switch is a real switch, role="switch", with its checked state carried for assistive technology, and its focus outline is 2px in The Isle Silver.
  • The choice is remembered on the visitor's own device, in localStorage under the key isle-theme. A remembered choice stands over the system preference, and nothing is sent anywhere. Pages with a toggle open in dark mode; where a page remembers the visitor's choice, the remembered choice stands over the opening default.
  • Touch: every control stands at least 44px tall, the Apple standard, and the switch stands 44px tall.

5Buttons

One form everywhere, in its two states

  • The form: sharp corners. Source Sans 3 at 14.5px, weight 700, letter-spacing .1em, the labels written in Title Case and set in capitals by the form, padding 14px by 22px, a 1px border and a 44px minimum height.
  • On: the button fills with The Isle Navy, its text The Isle Ivory, with a border of The Isle Silver in dark and The Isle Navy in light. On hover the fill lifts to Panel, the border unchanged.
  • Off: the button is transparent, with a border of The Isle Silver in dark and Page Silver in light, and its text in the mode's ink, The Isle Ivory in dark and The Isle Navy in light. On hover it fills with that silver, and its text turns to Deep in dark and The Isle Ivory in light.
  • Off and on are never carried by colour alone: the fill itself changes.
  • A button carries an icon only where the action has a true symbol of its own: the open book for reading, the tray arrow for a download. The icon stands at 17px before the words, with a 10px gap. Utility buttons carry no icon.
  • The assignment rule: the primary action on a page takes the on form, secondary actions take the off form, and utility actions stay text links, not buttons. Read is a button in the on form; Download is a button in the off form; Previous and Next are buttons in the off form; Full Size, Link to This Page, Tear Sheet and their kind are text links, and stay text.

6Fields

One form everywhere

  • A field stands at least 44px tall, in Source Sans 3 at 17px, on Panel in dark and Light Tint in light, with a 1px border of The Isle Silver at 30 percent in dark and Page Silver at 38 percent in light, curved corners of 6px and padding 8px by 12px.
  • On focus the border brightens to the mode's silver and the field shows the 2px silver focus outline. A field's label is a label in the house form.

7Links

Text links and the links inside text

A link inside text stands in silver, as this link stands in this sentence: The Isle Silver in dark and Page Silver in light, brightening on hover. It is never set in the body text colour, and never in a colour outside the seventeen.

A link that performs an action follows the assignment rule given with the buttons: an action with a button's weight takes a button's form, and a utility action stays a text link.

9The logo

Its parts, its clear space and its minimum sizes

  • The parts, named exactly: the logo is the island, the palm and the words together. The mark is the island and the palm, without the words. The palm alone is an element of the artwork, never the logo and never the mark. These names are used exactly, everywhere.
  • The anatomy of the mark, always: the crown is The Isle Green, the trunk is The Isle Gold, and the island, its waterline and the text follow the colourway. In colour artwork the island, the waterline and the text are The Isle Navy. In dark mode the island, the waterline and the text are The Isle Silver, and that does not change. The palm's green and gold never change.
  • The colourways are five: black, colour, silver, white, and silver with a colour tree. Colour is the form for light grounds. Silver with a colour tree is the dark mode form. Black and white are the single-colour forms, for print and reproduction where colour cannot go. Every colourway exists as a mark, a horizontal lockup and a vertical lockup, in PNG and vector, and copies for any use are issued from the masters in the master file set, never redrawn and never recoloured locally.
  • The mark is never redrawn, never recoloured outside the five colourways, never stretched, rotated, outlined or shadowed, and never placed on a ground that fights it.

The mark standing alone

  • The mark stands alone where the words are already given, or where they will not fit. Standing alone it carries the house in full: it is not a lesser form of the logo, and it is never set beside the words as a substitute for a lockup.
  • Standing alone, the mark takes the colourway its ground calls for, exactly as the logo does: the colour colourway on light grounds, silver with a colour tree on The Isle Navy and dark grounds, and black or white where a single colour is required.
  • The mark is never separated from its island, and the palm is never lifted out of it.

The palm

  • The palm's settled places are four: the artwork of the icon set; the seal, the palm centred above the name of the house; the profile picture off the site, whole and uncropped on white or The Isle Ivory; and the catalogue footer, where it flanks The Isle on both sides.
  • Two palms stand in the house's artwork and they are never interchanged: the windswept palm of the logo, which is the palm of the icon, the seal and the profile picture, and the Grand palm of the catalogue footer.
  • Beyond its settled places the palm is not decoration. In rare circumstances it may serve as an ornament; such a use is the exception, taken deliberately, and never becomes a habit or a motif.
  • The palm is never recoloured outside the colourways, never reversed, never cropped, and never redrawn.

Off the site

  • As a profile picture or avatar the mark stands in its colour colourway on white or The Isle Ivory, whole and uncropped, with its clear space.
  • As a seal it stands alone, in the colourway the ground calls for.
  • It is never set on a colour outside the seventeen.

Clear space

  • For a lockup: on all sides, three quarters of the height of the mark in the lockup, and nothing stands inside it.
  • For the mark standing alone: clear space of half the mark's own height on every side, and nothing enters it. The two measures, three quarters for the lockup and half for the mark, are not interchanged.

Minimum sizes

  • The horizontal lockup is never shown below 160px wide (40mm print).
  • The vertical lockup is never shown below 90px tall (22mm print).
  • The mark is never shown below 24px tall (6mm print). Below its minimum size the mark is not used; the icon serves at small sizes instead.

10Naming

How The Isle is named

  • The house and the place are called The Isle, with a capital T, in every use, including mid sentence and the possessive, The Isle's.
  • The street address never appears in anything created, presented, published or filed, including file names. Everything is just The Isle.
  • Regalia is the builder's plan and model name. It appears only inside source documents, never as the home's name in anything presented, written or published.
  • The Isle is a single residence, not a multi-structure facility, with unique named rooms, among them the Map Room, the Lanai, the Theater, the Studio and the Guest Suite.
  • Every colour is spoken and written under its full house name; a master colour's name is never shortened, in speech, in records or in code. The parts of the artwork are named exactly, as the logo section states them.

11The register

How The Isle speaks

The register: high end, elusive, exclusive, and at the same time barefoot luxury, down to earth and easy going. Nothing boasts and nothing sells. Quality is stated plainly and warmly; confidence comes through restraint and ease. The Isle is the ultimate destination, a cut above the rest, and says so by how it is made, not by claiming it. It is intentionally mysterious, complicated and robust, ultra in tropical luxury, and its complexity is embraced, never simplified away: stop trying to make it simple, it is complicated. The standard is luxury, refined elegance, the highest level for the most discerning guest.

  • The line of the house: "More than a place you come to, a place you stay." It is set in Playfair Display, italic, weight 500, wherever it stands. The words of the house line are exact and are never paraphrased.
  • Page titles: the splash is titled The Isle. Interior pages follow the form The Isle · <Name>: The Isle · The Build, The Isle · Reference Catalogues, The Isle · Secure. The 404 page carries Page Not Found · 1s1e.com.
  • No em dashes, anywhere, in messages or in anything created.
  • Words in use: dates are written in full, "November 22, 2026", and times as "7:21 AM"; a date and a time are joined by the middot. The middot is the house separator, in page titles, in statuses and in label groups, and a status stands in the form OPEN · <measure>. Labels are uppercase, letterspaced. Buttons and titles are in Title Case; body text and descriptions are in sentence case.
  • Units and numbers: measurements are feet and inches; elevations and levels are given to two decimal places; thousands are separated by commas; a figure and its unit are joined by a space.

In use

The register, shown. Three subjects, each set the house's way, and then the way the house never speaks.

The wrong version calls the home by the builder's plan name, exclaims, and claims an approval the record does not state.

12Correspondence

The closings

  • A personal closing takes one of two forms, both allowed: The Isle with M & D Silver beneath it, the imprint form, or The Isle alone.
  • House email, from the house's own address, is signed The Isle Staff: one line, in Title Case, standing as the whole closing.

13Print

Documents and print standards

  • A document follows this format: US Letter, margins of 56pt at the sides and the foot and 44pt at the top, no running head, and a running foot of one line in the label form naming the document "THE ISLE · <TITLE>" at the left of the column, the folio at the right.
  • Covers stand on The Isle Navy, with the mark in its silver with colour tree colourway.
  • A PDF carries MDS as its author of record, with producer and creator blank. Documents are issued at fixed names from the master file set.
  • In print, the mark stands in its black or colour colourway, on ivory or white stock. The catalogue PDFs are the standing example of finished print documents.
  • In print, the seventeen are built as CMYK from the tabulated builds in the colours section and proofed against the master values on the stock in use. The Isle Gold and The Isle Silver print as flat colour, never as metallic foil.
  • Gold in print takes the Page Golds: Page Gold dark on dark grounds and Page Gold light on light grounds. On pages the accent is silver, never the Page Golds.
  • The print minimums stand with the logo: the horizontal lockup never below 40mm wide, the vertical lockup never below 22mm tall, the mark never below 6mm tall.

14Motion, sound and imagery

How a page moves, sounds and shows

  • Motion everywhere follows one manner: gentle, slow and ambient. Fades run about a second; the poster crossfade is 1.2 seconds. Control transitions are short, a .25 second ease. Ambient cycles run from 10 to 21 seconds. Nothing that carries information moves. Hover brightens and never shifts the layout. Under reduced motion everything is still, and that is always honoured.
  • Sound follows the same discipline as motion: nothing sounds uninvited. Sound and music stand off by default, are offered deliberately, and a choice is remembered on the visitor's own device only.
  • The house is shown in its own photography, photographed as it stands, in full colour and untreated in character.
  • Where the subject is inspiration rather than the house, curated photographs from resorts and hotels around the world may be used, captioned as what they are.
  • Artwork leads where artwork is the subject, as the posters lead the splash.
  • No stock imagery, and no image of another house is ever presented as The Isle.
  • Image files for pages are issued from the masters at the size they will be shown, never enlarged beyond their master: photographs as JPEG or WebP, flat artwork as PNG or WebP, each stripped of metadata.

15The building materials

The sheet, the tokens and the palettes

No page hard-codes anything: everything created for the site takes its styling from the one master style sheet, published at /assets/the-isle-master.css, and links it as published. A page carries none of the values as its own copy: not the colour variables, not the font stacks or the type roles, and not the switch, the button and field forms. Where a section must depart from the master, the exception lives in that section's own folder, as that folder's only style sheet, holding only the exception. Images follow the same rule: one version of each image, at one address, referenced by every page that uses it.
