# THE ISLE · THE STYLE GUIDE

The single authority for everything created for The Isle and for 1s1e.com.
Icon, colour, font, design, spacing, rules, references and requirements,
without exception. Anything created for The Isle starts here; a value,
a name or a practice that is not in this guide is not settled, and
anything that departs from this guide is drift, to be corrected, not a
local variation. The companion style sheet is the-isle-master.css, which
carries the same values in working form. Settled November 22, 2026.

## 1. Name and the words

- The house and the place are called The Isle, with a capital T, in every
  use, including mid sentence and the possessive, The Isle's.
- The street address never appears in anything created, presented,
  published or filed, including file names. Everything is just The Isle.
- Regalia is the builder's plan and model name. It appears only inside
  source documents, never as the home's name in anything presented,
  written or published.
- The Isle is a single residence, not a multi-structure facility, with
  unique named rooms, among them the Map Room, the Lanai, the Theater,
  the Studio and the Guest Suite.
- The register: high end, elusive, exclusive, and at the same time
  barefoot luxury, down to earth and easy going. Nothing boasts and
  nothing sells. Quality is stated plainly and warmly; confidence comes
  through restraint and ease. The Isle is the ultimate destination, a cut
  above the rest, and says so by how it is made, not by claiming it. It is
  intentionally mysterious, complicated and robust, ultra in tropical
  luxury, and its complexity is embraced, never simplified away: stop
  trying to make it simple, it is complicated. The standard is luxury,
  refined elegance, the highest level for the most discerning guest.

### In use

The register, shown. Three subjects, each set the house's way, and
then the way the house never speaks.

- The house line. As the house speaks: "More than a place you come
  to, a place you stay." As it never speaks: "The ultimate luxury
  island escape. Book your unforgettable stay today!" The wrong
  version boasts, sells and exclaims; the line itself claims nothing
  and sells nothing.
- An entry, the Lot Viewer. As the house speaks: "The lot in full:
  its maps, boundaries, flood lines, survey and floor plans, layer
  upon layer." As it never speaks: "Everything about the lot — every
  map and boundary, even the tide charts." The wrong version sets an
  em dash, which never appears in the house's words; it is shown
  here once, as the fault itself. It also claims tide charts the
  page does not carry.
- The report, the Last Check. As the house speaks: "November 22,
  2026 · 7:21 AM. Documents: No new documents found. Action: Full
  check completed against the record. Nothing to publish." As it
  never speaks: "Wonderful news! Every Regalia permit is approved
  and ready!" The wrong version calls the home by the builder's
  plan name, exclaims, and claims an approval the record does not
  state.

- The line of the house: "More than a place you come to, a place you
  stay." It is set in Playfair Display, italic, weight 500, wherever
  it stands.
- Page titles: the splash is titled The Isle. Interior pages follow
  the form The Isle · <Name>: The Isle · The Build, The Isle ·
  Reference Catalogues, The Isle · Secure. The 404 page carries Page
  Not Found · 1s1e.com.
- No em dashes, anywhere, in messages or in anything created.
- Words in use: dates are written in full, "November 22, 2026", and
  times as "7:21 AM"; a date and a time are joined by the middot. The
  middot is the house separator, in page titles, in statuses and in
  label groups, and a status stands in the form OPEN · <measure>.
  Labels are uppercase, letterspaced. Buttons and titles are in Title
  Case; body text and
  descriptions are in sentence case. The words of
  the house line are exact and are never paraphrased.
- Units and numbers: measurements are feet and inches; elevations and
  levels are given to two decimal places; thousands are separated by
  commas; a figure and its unit are joined by a space.

## 2. Colours

Sixteen colours in total: the five masters and eleven working steps.
Every colour is spoken and written under its full house name; a master
colour's name is never shortened, in speech, in records or in code.

### The five masters

- The Isle Navy, #1d2a3b, RGB 29 42 59, CMYK 51 29 0 77. The one common blue for the whole
  group: grounds, ink, the logo's island, waterline and lettering in
  colour artwork, and the icon ground.
- The Isle Green, #46936a, RGB 70 147 106, CMYK 52 0 28 42. The palm crown, in the artwork
  only.
- The Isle Gold, #c9ab72, RGB 201 171 114, CMYK 0 15 43 21. The palm trunk, and gold on
  dark grounds: the coastline, headings, bright accents.
- The Isle Silver, #C0C0C0, RGB 192 192 192, CMYK 0 0 0 25. The dark mode silver: the
  logo's island, waterline and text in dark mode, and the splash's name.
- The Isle Ivory, #f0e8d5, RGB 240 232 213, CMYK 0 3 11 6. Text on dark grounds, the
  chart's curves and sounding figures on dark, and the light page ground.

### The Isle Navy, its steps

- Panel #22344a, RGB 34 52 74, CMYK 54 30 0 71: panels and cards on the navy ground, and the primary
  button's hover lift.
- Deep #152130, RGB 21 33 48, CMYK 56 31 0 81: the deepest tone: the page gradient's fall, wells and

- Ivory Dim #a89f88, RGB 168 159 136, CMYK 0 5 19 34: labels, captions and meta text on dark.

### The splash

- Poster Mat #dfe2e6, RGB 223 226 230, CMYK 3 2 0 10: the mat inside the poster frame.
- Terra #93a7c9, RGB 147 167 201, CMYK 27 17 0 21: the splash's quiet secondary tone.

The splash's tokens sit inside this family: its ground is The Isle Navy,
its name is The Isle Silver, its frame is equal to the ground, and its
shadow is cast in the ground's own RGB, 29, 42, 59.

The ground rule: in each mode, the theme colour, the body ground and the
page gradient's first and last stops are the one ground colour, The Isle
Navy in dark and The Isle Ivory in light, so the browser bars meet the
page without a seam at the top or the foot. A page is checked along its
whole length, at the foot as well as the top.

### The colours in use

- Text stands in its settled pairings: The Isle Ivory and Ivory Soft on
  The Isle Navy, Panel and Deep; The Isle Navy and Soft Ink on The Isle
  Ivory and Light Tint. Gold text is Page Gold, dark or light by the
  mode. The Isle Green lives in the artwork only; it is never a page
  text colour or a ground.
- Text pairings meet a contrast floor: 4.5 to 1 for body text, 3 to 1
  for large text. A pairing that falls short of the body floor is
  reserved for large text and labels. On the measured pairings, Dim
  Ink on The Isle Ivory (3.4 to 1) and Page Gold light on The Isle
  Ivory (3.1 to 1) are large text pairings only; every dark pairing
  in use passes the body floor.
- Colour in proportion: the ground carries the page, The Isle Navy
  in dark and The Isle Ivory in light, with Panel and Deep as its
  steps in dark and Light Tint in light. Text and surfaces take the
  ivory and ink steps. The Isle Gold is reserved to labels,
  eyebrows, rules, dots, the switch's knob, the coastline of the
  chart and small marks of that kind; it never fills a field or a
  panel and it is never a ground. The Isle Green appears only
  inside the artwork, and The Isle Silver in the artwork and where
  a colourway calls for it. A page that gives gold a field, or
  green a surface, is out of proportion and is corrected.
- Colour misuse is shown, not only stated: Plate B of the book shows
  The Isle Gold filling a field, The Isle Green used as a surface, a
  colour outside the sixteen set as a ground, and body text set in
  Dim Ink on The Isle Ivory, below the contrast floor.

## 4. Logo and the artwork

- The parts, named exactly: the logo is the island, the palm and the
  words together. The mark is the island and the palm, without the
  words. The palm alone is an element of the artwork, never the logo
  and never the mark. These names are used exactly, everywhere.
- The anatomy of the mark, always: the crown is The Isle Green, the trunk
  is The Isle Gold, and the island, its waterline and the text follow the
  colourway. In colour artwork the island, the waterline and the text are
  The Isle Navy. In dark mode the island, the waterline and the text are
  The Isle Silver, and that does not change. The palm's green and gold
  never change.
- The wordmarks are Playfair Display, weight 500, as master artwork;
  Playfair Display is The Isle's master logo font. Name treatments are
  placed as artwork files from the masters, never set as live page text
  and never redrawn. The /ref hero wordmark artwork is cream #f2eee3, a
  colour of the artwork itself, not a page ground.
- The colourways are five: black, colour, silver, white, and silver with
  a colour tree. Colour is the form for light grounds. Silver with a
  colour tree, a silver island, waterline and text under the green crown
  and gold trunk, is the dark mode form. Silver is the whole mark in The
  Isle Silver. Black and white are the single-colour forms, for print
  and reproduction where colour cannot go. Every colourway exists as a
  mark, a horizontal lockup and a vertical lockup, in PNG and vector.
- The master file set is the authority for the artwork. Copies for a site
  or for print are issued from the masters, never redrawn and never
  recoloured locally.
- The mark is never redrawn, never recoloured outside the five
  colourways, never stretched, rotated, outlined or shadowed, and never
  placed on a ground that fights it.
- Logo misuse is shown, not only stated: Plate A of the book shows
  the lockup stretched, squashed, recoloured outside the colourways,
  set on a ground that fights it and tilted, and the palm lifted out
  and set alone as if it were the logo.
- Clear space: on all sides of a lockup, three quarters of the height
  of the mark in the lockup, and nothing stands inside it.
- Minimum sizes: the horizontal lockup is never shown below 160px
  wide on screen or 40mm in print; the vertical lockup never below
  90px tall on screen or 22mm in print; the mark never below 24px
  tall on screen or 6mm in print.
- The error page pattern: the stacked vertical lockup in its silver
  colourtree form at min(720px, 86vw), centred, with no rule under it,
  then its heading, its sentence and the home link.
- Off the site, the artwork keeps its standards: as a profile picture
  or avatar the mark stands in its colour colourway on white or The
  Isle Ivory, whole and uncropped, with its clear space; as a seal it
  stands alone, in the colourway the ground calls for. It is never set
  on a colour outside the sixteen.
- No substitution: an image, file or element the user chose or supplied
  is never replaced without asking. If it cannot be used as it stands,
  that is said plainly and asked, never silently worked around.

### The mark

- The mark stands alone where the words are already given, or where
  they will not fit. Standing alone it carries the house in full: it
  is not a lesser form of the logo, and it is never set beside the
  words as a substitute for a lockup.
- Clear space: standing alone, the mark keeps clear space of half its
  own height on every side, and nothing enters it. The lockup keeps
  its own measure, three quarters, under the logo above; the two
  measures are not interchanged.
- Standing alone, the mark takes the colourway its ground calls for,
  exactly as the logo does: the colour colourway on light grounds,
  silver with a colour tree on The Isle Navy and dark grounds, and
  black or white where a single colour is required.
- The mark is never redrawn, never separated from its island, and the
  palm is never lifted out of it. Below its minimum size, 24px tall,
  the mark is not used; the icon serves at small sizes instead.

### The element

- The palm is an element of the artwork, never the logo and never the
  mark. It stands for the house only in the places settled here.
- Its settled places are four: the artwork of the icon set, in section
  3; the seal, the palm centred above the name of the house, as the
  style guide's own footer carries it; the profile picture off the
  site, whole and uncropped on white or The Isle Ivory; and the
  catalogue footer, where it flanks The Isle on both sides.
- Two palms stand in the house's artwork and they are never
  interchanged: the windswept palm of the logo, which is the palm of
  the icon, the seal and the profile picture, and the Grand palm of
  the catalogue footer.
- Beyond its settled places the palm is not decoration. In rare
  circumstances it may serve as an ornament; such a use is the
  exception, taken deliberately, and never becomes a habit or a motif.
- The palm is never recoloured outside the colourways, never reversed,
  never cropped, and never redrawn.

## 5. Type

- Two faces only, everywhere on 1s1e.com: Playfair Display for display,
  headings and serif lines; Source Sans 3 for body, labels and small
  text. Text pages load the faces once, with exactly this line:
  <link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Playfair+Display:ital,wght@0,500;0,600;1,500&family=Source+Sans+3:wght@400;600;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet" />
  The splash, whose lettering is artwork, loads Playfair Display 500
  only, for its fallback text. The fallback stacks are Georgia, Times New
  Roman, serif, and Avenir Next, Segoe UI, sans-serif.
- The six roles, at their master sizes:
  Display, Playfair Display 500, 64px. Serif line, Playfair Display 500
  italic, 19px. Heading, Playfair Display 500, 34px. Body, Source Sans 3
  400, 17px, line height 1.65. Label, Source Sans 3 600, 11px,
  letterspacing .34em, uppercase. Small, Source Sans 3 400, 10.5px,
  letterspacing .26em.
- Page measures, as patterns: the tagline is the serif line at
  clamp(19px, 2vw, 23px); report headings are labels in bright gold with
  the gold dot; entry titles are Playfair Display 600 at clamp(28px, 3vw,
  36px); entry and report body text is 16.5px at line height 1.5 to 1.55;
  row labels are 10px labels at letterspacing .24em.
- Catalogue measures, as patterns: the catalogue's line is Playfair
  Display at clamp(17px, 3vw, 23px), letterspacing .52em, uppercase; its
  hero wordmark is set at clamp(54px, 9vw, 88px); its volume headings sit
  at clamp(36px, 5vw, 50px).
- All fonts, sizing, spacing and weights match across the whole group.
  A page does not keep a private typography.

## 6. Spacing and layout

- The index composition is a split: two columns at 46 and 54 fractions,
  no outer frame, the page edge to edge. A single hairline divides the
  panels, inset 52px from top and bottom, never running
  off the screen. On phones the panels stack in order, and the divider
  and the chart are hidden.
- Panel measures: left panel padding 52px, 46px, 30px; right panel 34px,
  50px, 78px. The lockup sits atop the left panel, width
  min(252px, 72%). The tagline stands 26px below; the tagline
  rule, 56px by 1px, in gold at .75 opacity. The report begins 40px
  below.
- The left panel's foot carries 1s1e.com and the mode toggle.
- The chart sits in its own block in the open ground of the left panel,
  below the report, never behind text (section 7).
- Entries: a grid of a 96px numeral column and the text, gap 8px by 22px,
  padding 30px, 14px, 28px, divided by soft hairlines. The numeral is
  Playfair Display 500 at 58px. The status line under each entry is a
  10.5px label preceded by a 26px gold rule.
- The splash: a full viewport column, no scrolling. Masthead 112px, 78px
  on short screens, 100px on narrow, 74px on both. The wordmark is
  artwork, at clamp(38px, 6.7vmin, 56px) with letterspacing .34em, its
  set width 7.1652em. Below it a short centred silver rule, width
  clamp(140px, 28vw, 240px). The poster stage fills the remaining height;
  the frame's mat is Poster Mat, its padding clamp(10px, 3vmin, 28px);
  the frame is lifted 8px and throws a deep soft shadow in the ground's
  own RGB; the poster viewport is aspect 2 to 3, images cover,
  crossfading at 1.2 seconds, six seconds each, always starting on the
  first poster. All ten posters are preloaded after the first, so the
  rotation never shows a flicker or a blank frame. The splash is the
  masthead artwork over the framed poster
  and nothing else: no title extras, no buttons and no footer band stand
  on it.
- The catalogue manner: a hero band in The Isle Navy with a 3px gold
  top border, a centred ornament and the title in large serif; a footer
  on Deep, centred, the palm flanking The Isle on both sides, the
  imprint M & D Silver in spaced gold capitals, a thin gold line, then
  small text. The footer palm is the Grand palm, not the windswept
  palm.
- Nothing on any page requires sideways scrolling, at any width.

## 7. Chart

- The chart is the house's signature graphic: a fragment of an engraved
  nautical chart, drawn to one plate. It is set in its own block in open
  ground, never behind text, never colliding with it, never playful. One
  coastline, a fine double shore line in gold, entering and leaving the
  edges in the upper third, with a point and a bay, the land above it
  empty. No anchor, no hachures, no headlands, no names or labels. Its
  caption, SOUNDINGS IN FATHOMS, stands at the plate's top left. The
  plate's viewBox is 640 by 400.
- The shore line is this path: M-10 132 C 60 108, 110 84, 170 88 C 230 92,
  260 128, 320 134 C 380 140, 420 108, 480 104 C 540 100, 600 118, 650
  112. The inner shore line is the same path shifted 8 units toward the
  water, in gold and thinner. Three depth curves follow the shore as true
  parallel offsets, at +54, +108 and +162 units, dotted, in ivory.
- Soundings are taken along four survey lines run off the shore,
  deepening along each line in small steps: 2, 5, 11 and 19 down the
  first line, 3, 7, 14 and 23 down the second, 2, 6, 13 and 22 down the
  third, 4, 9 and 17 down the last, every figure clear of the curves.
  Sounding figures are Playfair Display, italic, 500, 13px.
- Motion is gentle and always on the water only: the depth curves drift
  on staggered cycles of 13, 17 and 21 seconds, the soundings pulse on
  10, 14 and 18 seconds, the coastline never moves. Under reduced motion
  the chart is still.
- PLATE I stands at the plate's bottom right, Playfair Display 600,
  10.5px, letterspacing .30em, in Page Gold: full Page Gold dark in the
  plate itself; as the index's page mark, Page Gold at 62 percent in
  dark mode and 68 percent in light, standing at the foot of the right
  panel. The plate carries no frame and no compass rose: it sits
  unboxed, as the chart sits on the site. On the index itself the chart
  block carries the caption only, and PLATE I stands as the page mark.
- Motion everywhere follows the same manner: gentle, slow and ambient.
  Fades run about a second; the poster crossfade is 1.2 seconds.
  Control transitions are short, a .25 second ease. Ambient cycles run
  from 10 to 21 seconds. Nothing that carries information moves. Hover
  brightens and never shifts the layout. Under reduced motion
  everything is still, and that is always honoured.
- Sound follows the same discipline as motion: nothing sounds
  uninvited. Sound and music stand off by default, are offered
  deliberately, and a choice is remembered on the visitor's own device
  only.

## 8. Report and the entries

- A status report is set in two headed groups. It rests on one
  universal check of the whole record, no matter how many records
  there ultimately are for the address. Each heading is a label in
  bright gold, preceded by the gold dot: a 5px circle in gold with a soft
  glow ring. The lead line under the first heading is Playfair Display
  600 at 22px, a date and a time. Rows are label and value pairs on a
  104px label column. A soft hairline divides the groups. The first group,
  headed THE LAST CHECK, states the last thing that happened; the second,
  headed WHAT HAPPENS NEXT, states the next thing that will happen,
  automatically.
- Records are set in rows: a full width table with a hairline under
  the header and under each row, header labels at 10px, letterspaced
  and uppercase, cells padded 8px by 10px, and figures right aligned
  in tabular numerals. A record's identifier is copied exactly and
  never shortened.
- An index entry carries a large pale numeral, a gold eyebrow label, a
  Playfair title, the exact description, and a quiet status line in the
  form OPEN plus its measure, and the whole block is the link. A work in
  preparation is shown ghosted, unlinked, in the same form.
- The entries as settled, stated in full because the instance is the
  standard: 01, eyebrow THE LAND, title Lot Viewer, to /build/lot/,
  "The lot in full: its maps, boundaries, flood lines, survey and floor

## 9. Rules for creating anything

How things are created:

- This guide governs everything created for The Isle, without exception.
  A value, a name or a practice that is not in this guide is not settled.
- No page hard-codes anything: anything created for the site takes
  its shared layer from the master style sheet, the-isle-master.css,
  published at /assets/the-isle-master.css, and links it as
  published. A page carries none of the shared values as its own
  copy: not the colour variables, not the font stacks or the type
  roles, not the switch, the button and field forms, and not the
  link colours. What a page keeps is its layout and what is
  specific to it.
- An exception to the shared layer is never written into a page:
  where a section must depart from the master, the exception lives
  in that section's own folder, as the section's style sheet,
  layered on the master, and the master itself is unchanged.
- Images follow the same rule: one version of each image, at one
  address, and every page that uses it references that copy, so an
  image is updated in one place and the update is universal. An
  image specific to a section lives in that section's folder.
- One approved sample precedes any full round of creative work. Nothing
  is built out in full on an unapproved direction.
- Samples are true samples: built from the real pages and their real
  style sheet, never hand-drawn approximations, and shown in every
  state the decision turns on, in both modes, at full quality.
- Alternatives differ in composition, organisation and visual world,
  never in cover imagery alone, and each is built out in full.
  Photography in samples is placeholder; The Isle's own imagery is used
  in finished work.
- No substitutions without asking: an image, a file, a product, a design
  element or wording the user chose or supplied is never replaced
  without asking. If the chosen thing cannot be used as it stands, that
  is said plainly and asked, never silently worked around.
- One composition is closed and never built: a separate login page
  followed by a vertical scroll of links.
- Corrections propagate everywhere the old value appears, and each is
  verified in place.
- A file that has been presented is never silently overwritten: a changed
  version is reissued under a fresh version name.
- Publishing is by git over SSH from the local clones, never by browser
  upload.
- A publish is verified the Bear LP way: the served source is fetched
  directly and hash compared against the published file, a nonexistent
  path is fetched so the served 404 is the page checked, and a dashboard
  hit is treated as final proof where a dashboard exists.
- Deletions are enumerated first, in the chat, and removed in one commit.
- Nothing is published, sent or deleted without the user's approval of
  the specific item.
- Decisions are put to the user as tappable choices, always, one question
  per message, with the recommendation named in the message.

What is required of anything created:

- Every page carries the full favicon set (section 3), without exception.
- The master colours (section 2) and the master type (section 5) only.
  A page does not keep private colours or a private typography.
- Both modes are always built and always checked, wherever a thing
  appears in two modes.
- Nothing requires sideways scrolling, at any width.
- Anything created is stripped of metadata. Where an author field is
  required it reads MDS. Nothing created carries any credit, attribution
  or other reference to its making.
- Every file created is audited against its source and this guide,
  fixed, and re-audited in full, repeating until one complete pass finds
  zero defects. Layout is verified in the rendered result, at the size
  the user will see it.
- A fix is reported as fixed only when it has been verified in the place
  the user will use it.
- Every page is checked at phone width, at iPad portrait and landscape,
  and at desktop width, in the engine the user actually uses, before it
  is called done.
- Every interactive element shows a visible focus, and every image
  carries alt text.

  copy in each master file set, updated in place as things change: this
  guide, THE-ISLE-STYLE-GUIDE.md, and the book drawn from it,
  documents/The-Isle-Style-Guide.pdf; the style sheet,
  the-isle-master.css; the design tokens, the-isle-tokens.json; the
  palettes, the-isle-palette.ase and the-isle-palette.gpl; and the
  swatch sheet, in swatches/. Their history is kept separately, in
  CHANGELOG.md in the set, never inside the documents themselves.
- This guide changes only on the user's word. Every change is entered
  in CHANGELOG.md, and a change is not complete until both master file
  sets carry it and the book drawn from it agrees.
- File and asset discipline: published files carry no version numbers.
  Copies for any use are issued from the masters only. The two master
  file set copies are kept identical, and a change is not complete
  until both sets carry it.
- The catalogue PDFs are finished print documents with their own settled
  interior design. The site presents them; the site palette does not
  govern their contents.

### Documents and print

- A document follows the book's own format: US Letter, margins of 56pt at
  the sides and the foot and 44pt at the top, no running head, and a
  running foot of one line in the label form naming the document
  "THE ISLE · <TITLE>" at the left of the column, the folio at the
  right.
- Covers stand on The Isle Navy, with the mark in its silver with colour tree colourway.
- A PDF carries MDS as its author of record, with producer and creator
  blank. Documents are issued at fixed names from the master file set.
- In print, the mark stands in its black or colour colourway, on ivory
  or white stock. The catalogue PDFs are the standing example of
  finished print documents.
- In print, the sixteen are built as CMYK from the tabulated builds
  in section 2 and proofed against the master values on the stock in
  use. The Isle Gold and The Isle Silver print as flat colour, never
  as metallic foil.

### Correspondence

- A personal closing takes one of two forms, both allowed: The Isle
  with M & D Silver beneath it, the imprint form, or The Isle alone.
- House email, from the house's own address, is signed The Isle Staff:
  one line, in Title Case, standing as the whole closing.

### Imagery

- The house is shown in its own photography, photographed as it stands,
  in full colour and untreated in character.
- Where the subject is inspiration rather than the house, curated
  photographs from resorts and hotels around the world may be used,
  captioned as what they are.
- Artwork leads where artwork is the subject, as the posters lead the
  splash.
- No stock imagery, and no image of another house is ever presented as
  The Isle.
- Image files for pages are issued from the masters at the size they
  will be shown, never enlarged beyond their master: photographs as
  JPEG or WebP, flat artwork as PNG or WebP, each stripped of metadata.

## 11. Technical standards

- The site is GitHub Pages, repository 1s1e/1s1e.github.io, branch main,
  published by git over SSH with the repository's dedicated key.
- The master style sheet is published at /assets/the-isle-master.css,
  byte identical to the copy in the master file set, and every page
  on the site links it.
- Where a section departs from the shared layer, its exception is
  kept in that section's own folder, as the section's style sheet,
  layered on the published master; the master itself is unchanged.
- Each image stands at one address and is referenced from there by
  every page that uses it, so an update to an image is made once
  and lands everywhere. An image specific to a section is kept in
  that section's folder.
- Visitor counting is GoatCounter, one counter per website; this site's
  counter is 1s1e, reported only on new activity, with the visits'
  locations.
- A page that counts by hand loads its tracker without counting, and
  counts each view once: its home view, each reader page as its own path
  and each download as its own event, so nothing counts twice.
- robots.txt allows the landing page and its /assets/ only.
- Locked pages are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, the key derived by
  PBKDF2-SHA256 at 250,000 iterations, decrypted in the visitor's browser
  by Web Crypto. Passwords are never published, filed or recorded.
- A locked page shows only its heading, the password field and the Open
  button; no content stands behind it. A wrong password is answered
  with one sentence only: "That password does not open this page."
- An image on a locked page is secure only when it is embedded inside
  the encrypted content. An image placed as its own file, even inside
  /secure/, is served to anyone who has its address, password or not.
- Pages with a toggle open in dark mode, and the toggle switches the
  whole page. The one exception: where a page remembers the visitor's
  choice, the remembered choice stands over the opening default, and
  otherwise their system preference is followed. Both modes are always
  built and always checked.
- The ground rule of section 2 is a check on every page: in each mode
  the theme colour, the body ground and the page gradient's first and
  last stops are the one ground colour, The Isle Navy in dark and The
  Isle Ivory in light, and the page is checked along its whole length,
  at the foot as well as the top.

## 12. Switch and the buttons

- The light and dark switch is one form everywhere: a pill, 117 by
  44px, fully rounded, on a Deep ground at 94 percent, with a 1px
  border of
  The Isle Gold at 55 percent, brightening to full The Isle Gold on
  hover. A sun glyph stands at the left, 11px in, and a moon glyph at
  the right, both in The Isle Gold at 85 percent.
- The knob is 54 by 32px, fully rounded, in The Isle Gold, carrying the
  glyph of the mode in use in Deep. It rests on the side of the mode in
  use and slides 51px to change, on a .25 second ease. The switch is a
  real switch, role="switch", with its checked state carried for
  assistive technology, and its focus outline is 2px in The Isle Gold.
  Where the switch sits is each page's own furniture.
- The choice is remembered on the visitor's own device, in
  localStorage under the key isle-theme. A remembered choice stands
  over the system preference, and nothing is sent anywhere.
- Touch: every control stands at least 44px tall, the Apple standard.
  The button carries a 44px minimum height,
  and the switch stands 44px tall, grown in proportion
  from its first form. No control on any page is smaller to touch
  than 44px.
- Form fields are one form everywhere: a field stands at least 44px
  tall, in Source Sans 3 at 17px, on Panel in dark and Light Tint in
  light, with a 1px border of Page Gold at 30 percent in dark and
  38 percent in light, curved corners of 6px and padding 8px by
  12px. On focus
  the border brightens to Page Gold and the field shows the 2px gold
  focus outline. A field's label is a label in the house form.
- The buttons are one form everywhere, the form the Reference
  Catalogues carried: sharp corners. Source Sans 3 at 14.5px, weight
  700, letter-spacing 0.1em, the labels written in Title Case and set
  in capitals by the form, padding 14px by 22px, a 1px border and a
  44px minimum height.
- One exception: the panel grid buttons of the Lot Viewer and the
  Shadow Study carry a smaller size, 10.5px on the Lot Viewer and
  13px on the Shadow Study, letter-spacing 0.05em and horizontal
  padding of 12px, so their longest labels sit on one line. Every
  other part of the form is unchanged. Within that exception, the
  Shadow Study's three sun buttons, Sunrise, Solar Noon and Sunset,
  carry their name and their time on two lines by design, the name
  on the first line and the time on the second, at the grid's 13px,
  so the three stand at one uniform height.
- A button carries an icon only where the action has a true symbol of
  its own: the open book for reading, the tray arrow for a download.
  The icon stands at 17px before the words, with a 10px gap. Utility
  buttons carry no icon.
- The master symbol list. Navigation: Home, the house; Back, the left
  arrow; Menu, the three lines; Close, the ×. Documents and media:
  Read, the open book; Download, the arrow into a tray; Print, the
  printer; Play, the triangle; Pause, the two bars. Places and
  records: Search, the magnifier; Calendar, the calendar page; Map,
  the pin; Layers, the stacked sheets; Secure, the padlock. States:
  Confirm, the checkmark; Warning, the triangle; Information, the i
  in a circle. Settings, the gear; Share, the arrow leaving a tray;
  Reset, the circular arrow. A new action joins this list only when
  its symbol is genuinely established, and every symbol is drawn in
  the button's own line style, never an invented variant. Actions
  with no established symbol, Hide, Show, Continue, Apply and their
  kind, carry no icon.
- Symbols are drawn to one specification: on a 24 by 24 grid, with a
  stroke of 2, round caps and round joins, in the currentColor of
  their setting, and no fill, save where an established symbol is
  solid by nature. The switch's sun and moon are the one exception:
  drawn small inside the pill, their stroke is 1.8. Set in a button,
  a symbol stands at 17px, as the book and the tray are drawn on
  /ref.
- Four symbols stand alone, replacing the words: Menu, Close, Search
  and Settings. A button carrying a symbol alone always keeps its
  name for assistive technology. Every other symbol stands beside
  its words.
- Project Home, the way back to The Build index, is a control with
  the home glyph alone, the house of the master symbol
  list, with no words. The glyph stands at 20px. Like every symbol
  standing alone, it keeps its name for assistive technology:
  Project Home.
- State pairs: a control that changes state carries the symbol of
  the state it is in. Menu and Close are one pair, the lines while
  the menu is closed and the × while it is open. Play and Pause are
  a pair, and the expand and collapse chevrons are a pair. The
  switch's sun and moon are state glyphs of the same kind.
- Off, the button is transparent, with a border of The Isle Gold in
  dark and Page Gold light in light, and its text in the mode's ink:
  The Isle Ivory in dark, The Isle Navy in light. On hover it fills
  with that gold, and its text turns to Deep in dark and The Isle
  Ivory in light.
- On, the button fills with The Isle Navy, its text The Isle Ivory,
  with a border of The Isle Gold in dark and The Isle Navy in light.
  On hover the fill lifts to Panel, the border unchanged. Off and on
  are never carried by colour alone: the fill itself changes.
- The assignment rule: the primary action on a page takes the on form,
  secondary actions take the off form, and utility actions stay text
  links, not buttons. The worked example: Read is a button in the on
  form; Download is a button in the off form; Previous and Next are
  buttons in the off form; the Reader's Download PDF and Try Again
  are buttons in the off form; Full size, Link to this page, Copy
  link, Hide and their kind are text links, and stay text.
- A link inside text stands in Page Gold, dark or light by the mode,
  and brightens on hover. It is never set in the body text colour, and
  never in a colour outside the sixteen. A link that performs an action
  follows the assignment rule above.
